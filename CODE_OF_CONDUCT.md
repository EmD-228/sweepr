# Code of Conduct

Sweepr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, welcome newcomers and people whose first language is not English, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately rather than replying in public. On GitHub, reach [@EmD-228](https://github.com/EmD-228) through the contact details on their profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
